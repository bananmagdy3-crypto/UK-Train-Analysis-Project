🚂 UK Railway Dataset Analysis & Interactive Power BI Dashboard
📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on performing a comprehensive exploratory and statistical data analysis on the UK Railway Dataset[cite: 1]. We utilize Python for data cleaning and preprocessing, followed by building an advanced, interactive Power BI dashboard[cite: 1]. The core objectives are to analyze train punctuality, identify root causes of delays (e.g., signal failures, weather conditions), evaluate ticket sales patterns, and deliver actionable operational insights[cite: 1].

🎯 Core Objectives & Analytical Questions
To ensure our analysis and dashboards address the core business objectives, our project addresses the following key areas:

Punctuality & Delay Analysis: Evaluating the percentage of delayed trains versus on-time arrivals and identifying primary root causes (signal failures, technical issues, weather conditions)[cite: 4].

Route & Station Performance: Determining which departure and arrival routes experience the highest traffic volume and most frequent delays[cite: 4].

Ticket Sales & Passenger Behavior: Analyzing how ticket classes (Standard vs. First Class), purchase types, and payment methods impact sales volume and refund requests[cite: 4].

Financial & Operational Impact: Calculating total revenue across routes and correlating delays with refund claims and customer compensation trends[cite: 4].
